## 1. How to Log In (Mobile & Desktop)

### Step 1: Open the Admin Login Link
Open any browser (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, etc.) on your phone or computer and visit:
👉 **[https://prettyvision.in/admin/login](https://prettyvision.in/admin/login)**

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials
- **Email Address**: Your registered admin email
- **Password**: Your admin password
- Click / tap the **"Sign In"** button.

> [!TIP]
> **Save to Phone Home Screen (Like an App)**:  
> On your phone in Chrome or Safari, tap the **Share** or **Menu (`⋮`)** icon and select **"Add to Home Screen"**. Now you will have a one-tap shortcut icon for PrettyVision Admin on your phone just like a mobile app!

### 4.1 Managing the Website Footer & Navigation Links
📍 **Menu Tab:** `Website Footer`  
🌐 **Direct Link:** `https://prettyvision.in/admin/footer`

Everything at the very bottom of the website is controlled here. The page has 5 simple tabs:

#### 1. Brand & Logo Tab:
- **Logo Image**: Click **"Upload Logo"** or paste an image URL. *(Best format: PNG with transparent background)*.
- **Brand Description**: The small paragraph below the logo introducing the clinic. Type your text and click **Save Footer Settings**.

#### 2. Navigation Columns Tab (Quick Links, Services, Support):
- **Add a New Column**: Click **"+ Add Column"**, give it a title (e.g., `Help & Policies`), and click Create.
- **Add a Link Inside a Column**: Click **"+ Add Link"** under the column.
  - **Link Label**: What the customer sees (e.g., `Book Eye Test`).
  - **Link Destination (URL)**: Where it takes the customer (e.g., `/contact` or `https://wa.me/919594030310`).
  - *Tip: We included 1-click presets for common pages like Eyewear Catalog, Services, Brands, etc.*
- **Reorder Links**: Click the **Up (↑)** or **Down (↓)** arrows next to any link to move it higher or lower.
- **Delete a Link or Column**: Click the red **Trash Can** icon.

#### 3. Store Contact List Tab:
- **Store Address**: Full shop address (e.g., *Shop No 11, Agarwal Building, Market, Kalina, Santacruz East, Mumbai 400098*).
- **Phone Number**: (e.g., `095940 30310`). When customers tap this on mobile, their phone dialer opens automatically.
- **Email Address**: (e.g., `prettyvisionkalina@gmail.com`).

#### 4. Social Media Links Tab:
- Paste your profile links for:
  - **Instagram**: `https://instagram.com/prettyvision_kalina`
  - **WhatsApp**: Type your 10-digit number (e.g., `9594030310`)
  - **Facebook**, **YouTube**, **LinkedIn**, or **Website**
- Only social networks you fill in will show an icon. If you leave one blank, that icon is hidden automatically.

#### 5. Newsletter & Copyright Tab:
- **Newsletter Subscription Form**: Turn the switch **ON** or **OFF**.
- **Copyright Year**: E.g., `2026`.
- **Copyright Text**: E.g., `PrettyVision. All Rights Reserved.`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always click the big blue **"Save Footer Settings"** button at the bottom after making changes! You can preview your changes live on desktop and mobile right on that page.

### 4.3 Adding & Editing Products (Frames, Sunglasses, Lenses)
📍 **Menu Tab:** `Products Catalog`  
🌐 **Direct Link:** `https://prettyvision.in/admin/products`

#### To Add a New Product:
1. Tap the **"+ Add New Product"** button at the top.
2. Fill in the basic details:
   - **Product Title**: E.g., `Titanium Rimless Aviator Gold`
   - **Brand**: Select from the dropdown (Ray-Ban, Oakley, PrettyVision Collection, etc.)
   - **Category**: Select (Men, Women, Unisex, Kids, Sunglasses, Computer Glasses)
   - **Regular Price (₹)**: E.g., `2499`
   - **Discounted / Sale Price (₹)**: E.g., `1999` (leave blank if no discount)
3. **Upload Photos**:
   - Tap **"Upload Main Image"** to choose a photo from your phone or computer.
   - You can upload multiple angles (front, side, lifestyle).
4. **Product Attributes**:
   - **Frame Material**: Acetate, Titanium, Metal, TR90, Wood, etc.
   - **Frame Shape**: Aviator, Round, Wayfarer, Cat-Eye, Rectangle, Square, Geometric.
   - **In Stock Switch**: Toggle ON.
   - **Featured on Homepage Switch**: Turn ON if you want this item highlighted on the front page.
5. Tap **"Save Product"**. It is now live in your catalog!

#### To Edit or Delete an Existing Product:
- Use the **Search bar** at the top to search by product name or brand.
- Click the **Pencil (Edit)** icon to change price, photos, or description.
- Click the **Trash Can** icon if you want to delete an item.

## 5. Pro Tips for Mobile Phone Editing

📱 **How to make edits on your smartphone without any hassle:**

1. **Use Good Lighting for Product Photos**:
   - Take photos of glasses against a clean white, marble, or wooden background.
   - Wipe the camera lens before snapping the picture.
2. **Direct Photo Upload**:
   - When you tap **"Upload Image"** on your phone, you can choose **"Camera"** (take a photo right now) or **"Photo Library"** (select an existing photo).
3. **Auto-Formatting Phone Numbers**:
   - You don't need to worry about formatting. The admin panel automatically adds country codes (`+91`) and creates WhatsApp links for you.
4. **Single-Handed Saving**:
   - Every form has its Save button at the bottom and stays accessible on small phone screens without you having to scroll sideways.

## 6. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: I changed a photo or text, but when I open the website, I still see the old one. Why?
> **Answer:** Your mobile or computer browser caches (saves) images so websites load faster.  
> - **On Mobile:** Close the tab and open the website in an **Incognito / Private tab**, or refresh the page twice.
> - **On Computer:** Press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac) to do a hard refresh.

### Q2: What image formats can I upload?
> **Answer:** You can upload **JPG**, **PNG**, or **WEBP**. PNG is best for logos (transparent background), and JPG/WEBP is best for frame photos and clinic banners.

### Q3: How do I change my admin password?
> **Answer:** Tap your profile icon at the top right corner of the admin panel, select **"Account Settings"**, enter your new password, and tap Save.

### Q4: Will changing something in the admin panel break other websites on the VPS?
> **Answer:** **No, never.** The PrettyVision admin panel connects strictly to its own dedicated database. Making changes here will never affect any other software or process on your server.

### Q5: How do I log out safely?
> **Answer:** Tap the **Log Out** button at the very bottom of the admin menu or inside your profile dropdown.
